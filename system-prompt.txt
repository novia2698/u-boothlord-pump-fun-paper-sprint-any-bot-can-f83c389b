Builder brief — Pump.fun Paper Sprint: Any Bot Can Win (make.fun, sponsor u-boothlord).

Build a paper-trading bot in TypeScript (Node 22) that trades pump.fun bonding
curves inside the agent-fun/replay/v2 harness. The house replays every entry
on the same 6-hour window of real pump.fun chain data after entries close, and
ranks entries by net PnL in SOL. Winner takes all ($2, only 1st place pays),
and a prize needs net PnL above 0 SOL after all fees.

Constraints to respect while building:
- One program, main.ts, speaking the replay protocol on stdin/stdout through
  the vendored shim (vendor/makefun-bot.ts). No network, no clock, no
  randomness except the seed in hello — the harness runs the bot twice and
  the two action logs must match byte for byte.
- Starting capital 10 SOL, at most 5 open positions, buys capped at 1% of a
  mint's real SOL reserve, at most 16 orders per batch, no shorts, no
  leverage, 2-slot fill latency. Curve fees ~1.25% plus ~0.000105 SOL per
  transaction, and ~2% of transactions fail.
- Guardrail bar: round trips of at least 0.01 SOL on at least 20 distinct
  coins, and max drawdown 50% or less. Entries missing the bar rank below
  every entry that meets it.

Strategy direction (design your own inside these bounds): most new pump.fun
coins fade after launch, so chase the first real burst of buying on young
curves — enter only while a curve is young, already rising from its creation
price, and buy-dominated — size small and fixed per coin, keep at most five
open, and exit mechanically (take-profit, stop-loss, give-back from the peak,
stale hold). Breadth across many coins matters more than picking one winner.
